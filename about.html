About Our Association

Rooted in Community. United by Opportunity.

Society of Access Alumni Nepal (SAAN) is a nationwide, voluntary network bringing together young leaders who completed the English Access Microscholarship Program in Nepal.

Identity and Purpose

Who We Are

The English Access Microscholarship Program has provided English communication, digital literacy, and civic awareness to bright adolescents from socio-economically disadvantaged communities across Nepal. Over the years, graduates have stepped into higher education, community organizations, public institutions, and professional careers.

SAAN was established as an independent alumni-led body to ensure that the friendships, shared democratic values, and leadership abilities gained during the program continue to benefit local communities across Nepal.

Our association is newly formed in 2026, transitioning from the former Access Alumni Association Nepal (AAAN) to establish structured representation in each of the country's seven federal provinces. We are currently mobilizing alumni directories and organizing regional focal points.

At a Glance: SAAN

  • Form: Voluntary, youth-led alumni network
  • Target Cohort: Graduates of the Access Microscholarship in Nepal
  • Scope: 7 Federal Provinces and 77 Districts of Nepal
  • Heritage: Successor to Access Alumni Association Nepal (AAAN)
  • Governance: Executive Working Committee and 7 Provincial Leads
  • Status: Newly established in 2026; assembling nationwide roster
Our Guiding Purpose

Our Mission

To connect, empower, and mobilize English Access Microscholarship alumni across all provinces of Nepal through civic engagement, educational mentoring, leadership development, and mutual support.

Our Shared Horizon

Our Vision

An inclusive, empowered nationwide community of Nepali youth who use their education, voice, and voluntary energy to foster positive social change, educational access, and grassroots civic leadership.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

The shared commitments that define how SAAN members collaborate, lead, and serve.

1. Community Service

Believing that education carries a social responsibility to uplift public schools, mentor children, and support local community welfare.

2. Inclusion & Equity

Ensuring equitable participation regardless of geographic location, caste, gender, economic status, or cohort graduation year across all 7 provinces.

3. Integrity & Transparency

Operating with open communication, honest accountability, non-partisan principles, and transparent stewardship of all alumni resources.

4. Lifelong Learning

Encouraging ongoing intellectual growth, digital literacy, language proficiency, and professional curiosity among all members.

5. Solidarity & Empathy

Fostering lifelong friendships and standing alongside fellow alumni and communities in times of regional need or disaster response.

6. Innovation

Applying creative problem solving, digital tools, and localized teaching approaches to address persistent educational and social challenges.

Historical Context

Our History & Evolution

From the founding of the Access program in Nepal to our transition into SAAN in 2026.

2011

Launch of Access Microscholarships in Nepal

The English Access Microscholarship Program was inaugurated in Nepal through the support of the U.S. Embassy Nepal, with NELTA serving as the nationwide implementing partner. Deserving students from public schools began their transformative two-year journey.

2015

Access Alumni Association Nepal (AAAN) Formation

Graduating scholars organically came together to form the Access Alumni Association Nepal (AAAN), coordinating earthquake relief support, informal learning camps, and alumni gatherings in the Kathmandu valley and select districts.

2018 to 2022

Expansion to Regional Cohorts

As new Access cohorts graduated in eastern, central, and western districts of Nepal, alumni formed regional networks, sharing peer tutoring techniques, virtual learning sessions, and community library initiatives.

2026

Transition from AAAN to SAAN

To adapt to Nepal's federal landscape and ensure fair representation for alumni in all parts of the country, the network transitioned from the old AAAN to the Society of Access Alumni Nepal (SAAN). SAAN establishes dedicated Provincial Leads in all 7 provinces under a unified central executive committee.

Geographic Coverage

Federal Structure: 7 Provinces of Nepal

SAAN operates through designated Provincial Leads across every province of Nepal.

Koshi Province

14 Districts | Hub: Biratnagar / Dharan

Connecting alumni across Morang, Sunsari, Jhapa, and eastern hill districts.

Madhesh Province

8 Districts | Hub: Janakpurdham / Birgunj

Anchoring alumni communication across southern Terai corridor districts.

Bagmati Province

13 Districts | Hub: Kathmandu / Chitwan / Hetauda

National secretariat liaison, university student circles, and inter-provincial forums.

Gandaki Province

11 Districts | Hub: Pokhara / Gorkha

Connecting alumni in Kaski, Tanahun, and mid-Himalayan valleys.

Lumbini Province

12 Districts | Hub: Butwal / Nepalgunj / Dang

Mobilizing alumni networks across western Terai and mid-hill districts.

Karnali Province

10 Districts | Hub: Birendranagar (Surkhet)

Coordinating regional contacts in Surkhet and remote mountain communities.

Sudurpashchim Province

9 Districts | Hub: Dhangadhi / Dadeldhura / Mahendranagar

Far-western alumni networking, youth mentoring, and regional outreach.
