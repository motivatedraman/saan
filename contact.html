Engagement Pathways

Ways to Get Involved with SAAN

As a newly reorganized network in 2026, here is how you can connect with our evolving community.

For Access Graduates

1. Reconnect with the Network

Graduated from the English Access Microscholarship Program in Nepal? Send us your cohort year, graduation district, and current province to get verified on our registry.

Email to Reconnect
Grassroots Service

2. Volunteer in Your Province

Register your interest to volunteer. When our Provincial Leads launch community reading circles, student coaching camps, or cleanups, you will be contacted.

Sign Up via Form Below
Institutions & Schools

3. Educational Partnership

We welcome partnerships with public schools, libraries, civil society groups, and community educators to co-organize youth workshops and book drives.

Email Partnership Desk
Direct Communication

Official Channels

You can reach the Society of Access Alumni Nepal directly through our verified channels: email, Facebook, or Instagram.

Official Email Address societyofaccessalumninepal@gmail.com Primary inbox for inquiries, registrations, and proposals
Facebook Community Page facebook.com/Society-of-Access-Alumni-Nepal-SAAN Official community page for notices and regional updates
Instagram Presence SAAN on Instagram Visual stories, youth spotlights, and event photos

Provincial Routing

Inquiries are reviewed by the secretariat and routed to the corresponding Provincial Lead for follow-up:

  • * Koshi: Eastern Hub
  • * Madhesh: Southern Terai
  • * Bagmati: Capital & Central
  • * Gandaki: Himalayan Hub
  • * Lumbini: Western Terai
  • * Karnali: Mid-Western
  • * Sudurpashchim: Far-Western
