SAAN Reorganization

Notice: Society of Access Alumni Nepal (SAAN) is newly established in 2026, transitioning from the former Access Alumni Association Nepal (AAAN). We are currently compiling our nationwide registry and building provincial chapters before launching inaugural programs.

Organizational Evolution

Transition from AAAN to SAAN

Access graduates in Nepal were originally mobilized under the Access Alumni Association Nepal (AAAN). In 2026, alumni leaders initiated a structural transition to form the Society of Access Alumni Nepal (SAAN).

This revitalization introduces designated Provincial Leads across all 7 provinces to guarantee grassroots representation, transparent coordination, and sustained connection for every Access graduate.

Current Status: Actively assembling regional rosters and preparing foundational working committees.
Who We Are

One Community. Seven Provinces. A Shared Foundation.

Society of Access Alumni Nepal is a non-partisan, youth-led association connecting graduates of the English Access Microscholarship Program.

The Access program provided two years of transformative English language education, digital skills, and civic leadership training. As a newly organized association, SAAN is building the platform where alumni from Koshi to Sudurpashchim can collaborate on future education, mentorship, and community service initiatives.
